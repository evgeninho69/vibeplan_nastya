## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a sanctuary of calm focus tailored for high school seniors, college applicants, and students preparing for critical life transitions. It actively rejects toxic productivity culture—characterized by sharp corners, aggressive red counters, and alarming streak counters—in favor of gentle, sustainable pacing reminiscent of a personal paper journal kept on a sunny café table.

The aesthetic fuses Korean stationery minimalism with tactile Scandinavian warmth. Key tenets include:
- **Paper Craft Tactility:** Surfaces evoke heavy matte paper stock, milk glass, and washi-tape tabs rather than cold digital glass.
- **Supportive Tone:** The visual narrative behaves like an empathetic companion: encouraging pauses, celebrating minor completions, and reducing exam anxiety.
- **Gentle Hierarchy:** Spatial relief and tonal contrast replace hard dividing lines to guide the eye through dense study schedules without sensory overload.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in an organic tea-and-café metaphor, balancing grounded earthy neutrals with plant-derived accent tones:

- **Primary (`#4D6B4B` Matcha Deep / `#7A9A78` Matcha Mid / `#D8E6D7` Matcha Foam):** Represents steady focus, study blocks, and active task progress. Provides clear readability when used for key interactive elements while maintaining an organic mood.
- **Secondary (`#685E86` Lo-fi Dusk / `#A79DC4` Lavender Fog / `#EAE5F5` Lavender Mist):** Serves as a soothing accent for reflective moments, mood tracking, self-care breaks, and non-academic reminders.
- **Tertiary (`#3D322A` Espresso / `#614F44` Roasted Bean):** Supplies high-contrast typography, structural outlines, and grounding icons. It avoids cold pure black (`#000000`) in favor of rich, bookish ink.
- **Neutral Palette (`#FAF7F2` Cream Vanilla / `#F4EFE6` Oat Paper / `#EFE8DC` Warm Cardboard):** Forms the foundational background and layered surface containers, generating the feel of tactile uncoated paper.

Use tint pairs functionally: progress indicators pair deep matcha (`#4D6B4B`) with a foam track (`#D8E6D7`), while exams or rest reminders pair dusk lavender (`#685E86`) against mist backgrounds (`#EAE5F5`).

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans is utilized across all tiers for its open counters, humanist letterforms, and warm geometry. The typeface captures an approachable editorial sensibility while retaining legibility in dense timetable grids.

- **Headlines & Day Markers:** Set in medium-to-bold weights with slight negative tracking to establish a clear anchor point for daily plans.
- **Body & Notes:** Set with generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to mirror spacious handwritten diary lines, keeping long study reflections easy to scan.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered with slight positive tracking to ensure small metadata (durations, subject tags, dates) remains crisp against tinted washi-tape style backgrounds.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows an adaptive fluid grid structured around the concept of desk organizing trays and notebook spreads:

- **Mobile Viewports (< 768px):** Single-column stack with `margin` of `1rem` and internal element gaps constrained to `space-md`. Daily priority cards, checklist items, and Pomodoro timers flow sequentially to limit visual fatigue.
- **Tablet Viewports (768px - 1024px):** 6-column grid with `margin-md` of `2rem` and `gutter-md` of `1.5rem`. Enables a two-pane layout: schedule timeline alongside actionable task buckets.
- **Desktop & Widescreen (> 1024px):** 12-column grid capped at a maximum container width of `1240px` to maintain comfortable eye-tracking distance. Features a fixed-width left navigation rail reminiscent of binder tabs, a primary daily/weekly planner canvas, and an ambient contextual drawer for habit reflections.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is modeled after physical desk objects resting on a smooth wood or linen work surface, using soft ambient diffusion rather than stark digital drops:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Rendered in `#FAF7F2` (Cream Vanilla). No elevation or border.
- **Level 1 (Paper Cards & Modules):** Rendered in `#F4EFE6` or pure `#FFFFFF` with a warm tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(61, 50, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(61, 50, 42, 0.02)`. Border is set to an ultra-soft paper stroke: `1px solid rgba(61, 50, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Sticky Notes, Floating Action Elements):** A raised stationery card effect: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(61, 50, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(61, 50, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Tactile Pressed State:** Interactive cards depress upon click/touch with a scale of `0.985` and reduced shadow radius, simulating pressing down on cardstock.

## Shapes
The shape language uses pill-shaped forms and generous container radii to cultivate a soft, unhurried interface.

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Employ `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem / 24px) to `rounded-3xl` (2rem / 32px) corner radii, eliminating aggressive points and giving components an organic, pebble-like presence.
- **Buttons, Chips, and Form Inputs:** Standardized on pill silhouettes (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for single-line controls, ensuring a friendly and approachable feel.
- **Stickers and Washi Tabs:** Use asymmetric corner curves (e.g., top edges curved, bottom subtly softened) to reinforce the digital scrapbook vernacular.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Matcha pill (`#4D6B4B`) with cream text (`#FAF7F2`). Hover moves subtly to `#3F583D`. Focus displays a faint 3px matcha halo (`rgba(77, 107, 75, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary / Soft Action:** Oatmeal base (`#EFE8DC`) with roasted bean text (`#3D322A`), providing low friction for secondary choices like "Add Note" or "Reschedule".
- **Ghost:** Transparent background with `#614F44` typography and subtle background fill on hover (`#F4EFE6`).

### Cards & Timetable Blocks
- Structured on `#FFFFFF` or `#F4EFE6` surfaces with `rounded-3xl` corners and `space-lg` inner padding.
- Study session blocks leverage pastel status washes: completed tasks turn matcha mist (`#D8E6D7`), rest blocks utilize lavender mist (`#EAE5F5`), and focus countdowns rest on warm oat (`#FAF7F2`).

### Chips & Subject Tags
- Height constrained to `32px` with full pill radiuses.
- Backgrounds use gentle tints: Matcha (`#D8E6D7`), Lavender (`#EAE5F5`), or Vanilla Cream (`#EFE8DC`). Text sits in matching deep shades (`#4D6B4B`, `#685E86`, `#614F44`).

### Checkboxes & Completion Items
- Checkboxes are large (`22px`), soft rounded squares (`6px` radius).
- Unchecked: `#EFE8DC` background with `1.5px` border in `#614F44`/20%.
- Checked: Transition to solid `#4D6B4B` with an animated smooth white checkmark. Completed task labels transition gracefully to roasted bean tint (`#614F44`/60%) with a delicate strikethrough.

### Input Fields
- Enclosed in full-pill bounds (`padding: 0.75rem 1.25rem`) on `#FFFFFF` with an inset paper boundary: `1px solid rgba(61, 50, 42, 0.1)`.
- Active focus state replaces borders with an organic matcha line (`#7A9A78`) and a 3px diffused outer ring (`rgba(122, 154, 120, 0.2)`).

### Progress & Habit Bars
- Track heights are generous (`8px` to `12px`) with pill end-caps.
- Background rails use tinted foam (`#D8E6D7` or `#EAE5F5`). Active fills use soft gradient transitions between `#7A9A78` and `#4D6B4B`, removing numerical pressure and highlighting consistent momentum.
